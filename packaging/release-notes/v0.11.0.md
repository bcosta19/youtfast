# YouTFast 0.11.0

Device sync now finds your other computers on its own, members-only videos are marked, playback memory is reclaimed on stop, and a crash on multibyte YouTube metadata is fixed.

## Included

- **Device sync** finds your other computers by itself: with **Other device address** empty, YouTFast asks the local network which devices share the pairing key, asks the `tailscale` and `netbird` clients for their peers, and devices that synced share the other computers they know, so meeting any one computer is enough to meet them all. It syncs with every computer answering on the same **Listen port** with the same key, searching again every 10 minutes, when known devices stop answering, and on **Sync now**. Sync also starts at launch and a few seconds after its settings change, so a newly installed computer pulls the others' library as soon as the key is pasted. Local-network discovery needs incoming UDP on the listen port as well as TCP; the Flatpak cannot run the clients but still finds devices on the local network and through shared peers.
- `curl -fsSL https://youtfast.pages.dev/install.sh | sh` on Arch no longer quits silently at pacman's `Proceed with installation? [Y/n]`: the question now reads its answer from the terminal instead of the piped script, and without a terminal the installer asks for `--yes`.
- Marked members-only videos with a "Members only" badge on their thumbnails and on the watch page, instead of presenting them like any other video.
- Prevented a worker panic when parsing YouTube metadata or dates containing multibyte UTF-8 characters (such as `ç`).
- Reclaimed playback memory after resolution switches and closing video tabs instead of letting Linux RAM usage climb across repeated plays: tuned glibc's arena and mmap thresholds, trimmed freed heap pages on stop/unload, cleared mpv's cached yt-dlp JSON on stop, and deleted the video framebuffer plus flushed `vo_libmpv`'s internal video textures once playback confirms idle.

## Packages

Linux x86_64 native, Flatpak and Arch; Windows x86_64 installer and portable
ZIP; macOS 15+ Apple Silicon; locked Rust sources, matching native sources,
notices, channel manifests and SHA-256 checksums. Native Linux needs glibc
2.39+, libmpv with Lua (0.42+ recommended; older versions start videos a few
seconds late), and current yt-dlp and Deno; Windows and macOS bundle libmpv
but require yt-dlp and Deno separately. Flatpak includes its playback and
extraction tools.

## Not included

- Flathub or AUR publication, submission to the official Winget catalog,
  Windows publisher signing (Authenticode) or macOS notarization.
- Intel macOS, Windows ARM64 or Linux ARM64 packages.
- Bundled yt-dlp and Deno in Windows packages, or a bundled Cast helper.

## Known limitations

- Windows and macOS automated builds do not establish interactive playback,
  graphics or credential-store coverage; platform QA remains tracked in
  [#5](https://github.com/bcosta19/youtfast/issues/5) and
  [#6](https://github.com/bcosta19/youtfast/issues/6).
- The Arch package's `mpv>=0.42.0` dependency does not stop pacman from
  installing it beside Arch's `mpv 1:0.41`, because of the package epoch; with
  0.41, videos start a few seconds late.
- Windows Credential Manager may reject sessions larger than 2560 bytes.
  Browser-session import additionally needs Python and its yt_dlp module.
- Flatpak cannot read host browser profiles; use cookies.txt or stay signed out.
  The standalone Flatpak bundle has no automatic update remote.
- Casting remains unqualified on physical Chromecast and Google TV devices.
  YouTube changes can break playback extraction; keep yt-dlp current.
