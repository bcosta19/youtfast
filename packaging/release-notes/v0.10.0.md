# YouTFast 0.10.0

Choose from twelve interface styles, sync your local library across devices, and tune local video recommendations.

## Included

- **Interface style** selector under **Settings → Appearance** with twelve selectable visual styles (**Cupertino Quiet** as the default, **Liquid Glass**, **Cinema Minimal**, **Warm Studio**, **Adwaita (GNOME)**, **Fluent Mica**, **Industrial (Braun)**, **Technical (Tiling)**, **Tactile Modern**, **Material Tonal**, **Paper**, and **Classic**), tailoring dark/light palettes, tonal surface elevation, hairline or sharp 1 px borders, tactile bevels, floating player dock, rounded miniplayer and pop-out PiP corners, sidebar width animation, and transition timing live while keeping custom JSON and Omarchy palettes intact.
- **Device sync** under **Settings → History**: optionally sync your device subscriptions, watch history (including resume positions), Watch later, device playlists, hidden videos, and blocked channels directly between your computers over Tailscale, NetBird, or a private local network using an encrypted connection authenticated by a shared pairing key, plus a **Sync devices now** command-palette action. Accounts, sessions, alert timestamps, and settings stay on each computer.
- Home → **For you** now offers persistent **Local recommendation settings**: duration, channel discovery or familiarity, interests, muted title terms, and priority for recent uploads or fewer views. Signed-in users can choose the local ranking instead of YouTube's. An optional first-run search sends up to three interests to YouTube without signing in when no eligible device history exists; muted terms stay on the device.
- Command palette (`Ctrl+K`, `Cmd+K` on macOS): a localized fuzzy list over sidebar views, page and player actions (`Refresh page`, `Toggle sidebar`, `Back`, `Forward`, `New video tab`, `Play` / `Pause`, `Mute` / `Unmute`, `Theater mode`, `Full screen`, `Mini player`, `Captions`, `Audio only`, `Transcript`, `Loop`, `Next video`, `Previous video`), Settings groups (`Appearance`, `Playback`, `Sidebar`, `History`, `Notifications`, `About`), recent searches, and recent watch history entries, navigated with `↑`/`↓`, `Enter`, `Esc` or the mouse without making network requests.
- Pop-out picture-in-picture OS window (`P` or the player/miniplayer control), with play/pause, close, and return-to-main-window controls alongside the existing in-window mini player. Always-on-top is requested where supported; on Wayland, use compositor rules for `youtfast-pip`.
- Optional DeArrow integration (both switches off by default under **Settings → Playback**): **Community titles (DeArrow)** sends only a 4-hex-digit SHA-256 prefix to `sponsor.ajay.app` and shows the original title on hover; **Community thumbnails (DeArrow — sends video ID)** sends the video ID to `dearrow-thumb.ajay.app` and keeps the YouTube thumbnail until the community image is ready. A winning vote for the original keeps YouTube branding. Turning thumbnails off cancels queued thumbnail requests; turning both options off also cancels queued title and thumbnail metadata lookups. DeArrow results are kept in memory, never saved to disk.
- Local device-only playlists stored in `library.json` (`local-` IDs, up to 200 playlists of 5,000 videos each), available in the **Playlists** sidebar entry both signed out and signed in (marked **On this device**), with create, rename, delete, reorder, **Play all**, and quick saving from the watch page's **Save** menu and video card menus.
- Playlist save menus scroll through all destinations, and device playlists explain when creation or adding videos is unavailable at capacity. **Play all** follows playlist order across regular videos and Shorts.
- Local feed filtering on Home and Subscriptions: **Not interested** and **Don't recommend channel** actions in card menus (excluding channel pages) hide individual videos (up to 5,000) and block channels (up to 1,000) on this device, excluding them from Home, Home's Shorts shelf, Subscriptions, and local recommendations (never from search results or channel pages); a persistent **Hide watched** chip toggles hiding watched videos on Home and Subscriptions; and **Settings → History** lists hidden video counts (**Clear**) and blocked channels (**Unblock**).
- Per-category SponsorBlock controls in **Settings → Playback** (`Sponsor`, `Self-promotion`, `Interaction reminder`, `Intro`, `Outro`, `Preview`, `Non-music section`, `Filler`), each configurable as **Skip automatically**, **Show skip button**, **Only mark on the seek bar**, or **Ignore**, with defaults preserving existing behavior. SponsorBlock requests now include `mute` segments, automatically muting playback inside `Skip automatically` mute segments and restoring audio on exit, stop, video change, or manual mute toggle; TV casting skips `Skip automatically` skip segments.
- Versioned JSON library backup and restore (**Settings → History → Back up library**: **Export library (JSON)** / **Import backup**, preserving device subscriptions, watch history with resume positions, and Watch later while leaving out accounts, sessions, and avatar URLs; also accepted by the Takeout picker) plus OPML 2.0 subscription export (**Export OPML**) for valid YouTube channel IDs. Exports cannot replace the app’s live library or settings files, and JSON exports must fit the restore limits.
- Remember playback speed globally across videos and restarts, with a **Remember for this channel** switch in the player's speed menu for per-channel overrides (channel speed takes precedence over global speed; temporary 2× hold-to-speed-up never overwrites stored speeds), plus **Settings → Playback → Per-channel playback speeds** to clear saved channel entries. Live streams stay at 1× at the live edge, while rewound live streams keep speed controls and 2× hold available to catch up and reset to 1× once back at the edge without overwriting saved speeds.
- Refined recent and core UI components across styles: structured cards and compact remote controls in **Cast to TV**, status pill badges, styled monospace inputs and segmented interval selectors in **Device sync** and **Notifications**, colour-coded indented category rows in **SponsorBlock**, grouped category headers and keycap badges in the **Command palette**, styled inline playlist creation/rename inputs, and a distinct toggle chip for **Hide watched**.
- Stop submitting releases to the official Winget catalog; the maintainer has not accepted Microsoft's Contributor License Agreement. Windows setup and portable downloads, Homebrew updates and website deployment remain available.
- Playback progress saves no longer copy unchanged device playlists.
- Search can be submitted by clicking the magnifying glass in every interface style. The search hint and accent badges are more readable in built-in palettes.
- The floating playback dock adapts to narrow players without overlapping controls. Device-sync errors wrap, and Cast and sync status badges expose their text to assistive technology.
- The command palette blocks the mouse Back and Forward buttons while it is open.
- The command palette offers video full screen only on the watch page.
- Dismissing the command palette returns keyboard focus to the previous control when it is still available.
- Enter runs the highlighted command for the updated palette query, not the search behind it, and keeps an unmatched query editable.
- Tab and Shift+Tab move the command palette highlight forward and backward without leaving the search field.
- Keep account subscriptions pageable when all channels on the loaded pages are blocked.
- Keep hidden, blocked and filtered watched Shorts out of the Home shelf's Up next list.
- Apply channel blocks to signed-in Shorts when YouTube supplies an owner ID for the Short or another copy of it. Shorts without an identifiable owner can still be hidden individually.
- Rebuild local For you recommendations after hiding or restoring videos or channels, without requiring a manual refresh.
- Exclude hidden and blocked watches from the channel preferences used to rank local recommendations.
- SponsorBlock restores local audio even if its segment list refreshes during auto-mute.
- Unmuting or changing volume overrides SponsorBlock auto-mute for all overlapping segments.
- SponsorBlock mutes the full duration of a mute segment, including segments shorter than 300 ms.
- Changing a SponsorBlock category invalidates pending TV skip checks so they cannot seek using the old settings.
- On Windows, Google Cast now finds a `catt` installed with `pip install --user catt` or `pipx install catt`, even though those folders are not on the `PATH` a desktop application inherits; a running YouTFast picks it up at the next search.
- The TV picker no longer reports a missing `catt` as the search's error, which masked why no YouTube TVs were found: it is now guidance beside the results, and the IP-address and TV-code routes stay available without it.
- The **Cast to TV** picker no longer clips its TV-code section behind a too-short dialog: it now sizes to its full content in every language, and only scrolls when the screen is too short for it.
- Updated `url` to 2.5.9 to fix [RUSTSEC-2026-0592](https://rustsec.org/advisories/RUSTSEC-2026-0592).

## Packages

Linux x86_64 native, Flatpak and Arch; Windows x86_64 installer and portable
ZIP; macOS 15+ Apple Silicon; locked Rust sources, matching native sources,
notices, channel manifests and SHA-256 checksums. Native Linux needs glibc
2.39+, libmpv with Lua (0.42+ recommended; older versions start videos a few
seconds late), and current yt-dlp and Deno; Windows and macOS bundle libmpv
but require yt-dlp and Deno separately. Flatpak includes its playback and
extraction tools.

## Not included

- Flathub or AUR publication, submission to the official Winget catalog,
  Windows publisher signing (Authenticode) or macOS notarization.
- Intel macOS, Windows ARM64 or Linux ARM64 packages.
- Bundled yt-dlp and Deno in Windows packages, or a bundled Cast helper.

## Known limitations

- Windows and macOS automated builds do not establish interactive playback,
  graphics or credential-store coverage; platform QA remains tracked in
  [#5](https://github.com/bcosta19/youtfast/issues/5) and
  [#6](https://github.com/bcosta19/youtfast/issues/6).
- The Arch package's `mpv>=0.42.0` dependency does not stop pacman from
  installing it beside Arch's `mpv 1:0.41`, because of the package epoch; on
  0.41 playback relies on the retries above.
- Windows Credential Manager may reject sessions larger than 2560 bytes.
  Browser-session import additionally needs Python and its yt_dlp module.
- Flatpak cannot read host browser profiles; use cookies.txt or stay signed out.
  The standalone Flatpak bundle has no automatic update remote.
- Casting remains unqualified on physical Chromecast and Google TV devices.
  YouTube changes can break playback extraction; keep yt-dlp current.
