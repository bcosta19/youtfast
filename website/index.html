Common questions.

Do I need a Google account?

No. Your library stays on this device. An account is optional, and its session is kept only in the system credential store. This unofficial integration can trigger Google verification or sign-out; a secondary account is safer.

Is this an official YouTube app?

No. YouTFast is independent and unaffiliated with YouTube or Google.
