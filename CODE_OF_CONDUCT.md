# Code of conduct

YouTFast welcomes respectful contributions regardless of background, experience
or identity. Be considerate, focus criticism on code and ideas, and accept
constructive feedback. Disagreement is welcome; harassment is not.

Do not use insults, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment,
spam, or disclose another person's private information. Do not post credentials,
private account data or screenshots containing other people's personal data.
These expectations apply to issues, pull requests and project discussions.

The maintainer `@bcosta19` handles reports. For sensitive situations, request a
private contact channel without naming or exposing affected people publicly.
Reports should state what happened and provide relevant evidence privately.
The maintainer will handle reports with discretion and avoid retaliation.

Depending on severity or repetition, enforcement may include editing or removing
content, a warning, temporary restrictions, or exclusion from the project.
Maintainers and contributors are held to the same standards.
